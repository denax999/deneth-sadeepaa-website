I'm Deneth Sadeepa, known online as Denax — a YouTube content creator, researcher and visual storyteller.

My work lives at the intersection of curiosity and storytelling. I explore mysteries of the unknown, ancient legends, spirituality, meditation, psychology, and the hidden depths of the human mind, turning research into videos designed to make people pause, think and question.

I also collaborate with companies and individuals to create original videos around their chosen topics — focusing on the idea, research, structure and storytelling rather than simply editing footage.
